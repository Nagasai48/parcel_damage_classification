What is Parcel Damage Classification?

The global supply chain moves millions of packages daily. Unfortunately, transit inevitably causes wear, tear, and critical structural damage to packaging. Manually inspecting every box is impossible, leading to undetected damage, unhappy customers, and complex insurance disputes.

Deep Learning Vision

We utilize state-of-the-art Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) trained on thousands of warehouse images to identify dents, tears, water damage, and structural compromise in real-time.

Instant Reporting

Users simply upload an image of the parcel in question. Within seconds, the system returns a confidence score dictating whether the package is 'Intact' or 'Damaged'.

Enterprise Management

Administrators have complete oversight of user activities and historical prediction analytics, making supply chain auditing transparent and data-centric.
